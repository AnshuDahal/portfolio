WASHINGTON UNIVERSITY IN ST. LOUIS / RESEARCH

Good answers need
good evidence.

MyeloSeq · Clinical-report chatbot evaluation

Helping make complex cancer reports understandable starts with a harder question: can we trust what the chatbot says?

I built a RAGAS evaluation workflow to compare retrieval approaches, inspect grounding failures, and measure what an overall score can miss.

PythonLlamaIndexRAGASRAG
Read the project note ＋

I compared metadata-enabled retrieval and reranking using answer relevancy, faithfulness, context precision, and context recall. Breaking results down by topic and difficulty exposed weaknesses on more complex questions.

The takeaway: finding a relevant passage is only part of answering a question. The retrieved evidence also needs to be complete.

DDI Dermatology AI Fairness

A reproducible research workflow for training, calibration, validation, and reporting across skin-tone groups using DDI and Fitzpatrick17k.

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • EfficientNet-B3
  • Grad-CAM
  • pandas

Result: Improved reported FST V–VI AUC from 0.61 to 0.77 and reduced false-negative rate from 0.88 to 0.42 across a 10-fold evaluation plan.
